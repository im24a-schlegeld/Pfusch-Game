The game remains an unbranded stylized sport motorcycle; the reference establishes mechanical proportions, not a claim of a licensed exact replica.

## Root causes and repair

The old rear tire was a circular torus. Increasing width also increased radial sidewall thickness, shrinking the visible rim. Both wheels used the same 325 mm radius and the wheelbase was 1530 mm. Fork covers used separate endpoints that did not follow the main fork line. The original flattened-tube fender crossed the tire; its initial arc replacement was too long.

Both rims now use a 215.9 mm nominal radius. Crown/sidewall profiles are independent of tire width: front outer radius 299.9 mm, rear 320.4 mm. Axles are 1441 mm apart and each is positioned at its tire radius. Rear-wheel ground contact remains the wheelie pivot. Front discs are twin 160 mm radius annuli, rear 110 mm, with stationary calipers. Fork tubes/covers share their axis. The fender covers 1.61 radians with at least 27 mm radial crown clearance. Front and side surfaces meet at a shared curved nose boundary, with a separate lower panel/belly pan and visible radiator.

The Töffli front axle moved forward by 120 mm, from z=-0.58 to -0.70, around the retained steering-head target. Its wheel and fender move together; tire radius, fender clearance, frame and immutable rider dimensions remain unchanged.
