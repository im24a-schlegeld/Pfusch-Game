# Classic moped riding asset

User-supplied archive: `moped.zip`; imported geometry: `moped.3ds`.
3DS SHA-256: `071c3326293c307854bc52cec2ae4b92cb0b9b4f08f7af062c89249d8f0f2632`.

Rebuild with `node scripts/build-moped-assets.mjs moped <source.3ds>` and then the project imported-bike dimension sync script. The supplied `.blend` file is not opened or executed.

- Convert Z-up/source −X-forward geometry to metres, +Y up, −Z forward, axle midpoint Z=0, wheels on ground. Proportional reference wheelbase: 1.10 m. This is an explicit game calibration, not an asserted manufacturer measurement.
- Original tyre centres establish axles/radii; supplied seat, bars and pegs establish rider contacts. No rider anatomy is included or changed.
- Omit the source bitmap atlas entirely, removing printed branding without inventing replacement artwork. Use separate neutral paint, rims, rubber, seat, mechanics and lamp materials.
- Slightly round the original `kutija` side airbox with a 0.85-source-unit edge field after two triangle subdivisions; preserve its location and outline.
- Remove the `torbe` side bags and their `nosacTor` support rack. Keep the seat and its strap, mirrors, engine, controls and exhaust.
- Restyle the original tank, side cover and mudguard outlines modestly, and reshape the headlamp into a wider oval. Wheel meshes, handlebars, seat and all rider contact anchors stay unchanged.
- Rig independent wheels, steering/headlight, lower fork/fender, swingarm, and upper/lower portions of the supplied paired rear shocks.
- 9,526 source triangles; 10,228 before simplification after removing bags and rounding the airbox; 7,722 exported triangles across 19 material/rig parts, 256,832 bytes geometry.
