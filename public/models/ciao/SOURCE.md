# Ciao riding asset

User-supplied source: `PiaggioCiao.fbx` (kept outside the repository).
SHA-256: `7bba392d7ed09455763f7fde8102a52ba94415de39170e595e8b6d4256e7eeb6`.

Rebuild with `node scripts/build-moped-assets.mjs ciao <source.fbx>` and then the project imported-bike dimension sync script.

- FBX geometry is baked into a static Three.js mesh manifest and binary; no source code or animations execute.
- Canonical coordinates: metres, +Y up, −Z forward, axle midpoint Z=0, wheels on ground. Proportional reference wheelbase: 1.04 m. This is an explicit game calibration, not an asserted manufacturer measurement.
- Original front/rear tyres and cast wheels determine axle centres and tyre radii. Rider contacts derive from the supplied seat, grips and pedals. No rider anatomy is included or changed.
- Remove `Text001`, SVG logo `Curve001`, logo backing `Plane004`, and the complete rear number-plate mesh `Plane003`; omit all bitmap maps. Paint and rims remain separate configurable materials.
- Remove the remaining high rear rack/holder slab `Cube013`, its `Bolts011` fasteners and only the two disconnected 24-triangle support strips in `Cube009`. Keep that mesh's 340-triangle central seat/frame member and the continuous `Rear_Fender001` mudguard intact; all other rear parts remain.
- Fold the supplied `cable012` centre stand 1.30 radians around its upper hinge into riding position.
- Give the supplied frame shell/side cover broader, cleaner contours, widen the mudguards slightly and reshape the original headlamp into a lower, wider face. Wheel meshes, handlebars, seat and rider contact anchors stay unchanged.
- Separate front/rear spinning wheels, steering/headlight, lower fork/fender and fixed frame. The source is a hardtail: its rear wheel stays fixed to the frame, without invented rear suspension.
- 1,363,080 source triangles; 1,357,102 after lettering, number-plate and rear-rack/support removal; 20,567 exported triangles across 13 material/rig parts, 601,554 bytes geometry. Dense source tyre tread is reduced while retaining the round silhouette.

`outputs/model-audit/ciao.png` is the CPU-only side/front audit generated by `node scripts/render-motorcycle-audit.mjs ciao`. Source ownership/licensing remains with the user-supplied asset; no additional licence is inferred here.
